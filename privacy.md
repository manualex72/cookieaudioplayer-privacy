# Privacy Policy for CookieAudioPlayer

**Effective date:** \[Insert date before publishing]

CookieAudioPlayer ("the App") is developed by Manu Alex ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use it.

## Summary

CookieAudioPlayer is a local music player. It does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data to us or to any third party. All app data stays on your device.

## Information We Access

To function, the App requests the following device permission:

* **Music/Audio files (READ\_MEDIA\_AUDIO or READ\_EXTERNAL\_STORAGE, depending on your Android version):** Used solely to find and play audio files already stored on your device. The App does not upload, transmit, or share these files or their metadata (song titles, artists, album art) with us, with any server, or with any third party.

## Data Stored on Your Device

The App saves the following information locally on your device only, using Android's standard app storage. This data is never transmitted anywhere:

* Your favorite songs
* Playlists you create and their contents
* Recently played history
* Equalizer settings, presets, and custom presets
* App preferences (theme, volume, playback settings)

Uninstalling the App permanently deletes this data. We have no access to it at any time.

## Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any analytics, advertising, or tracking services. It does not connect to the internet for its core functionality.

The App uses the following open-source library for local functionality only:

* **Coil** — used to load and display album artwork already embedded in your local music files. This does not involve any network request or data transmission.

## Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.
